\section{Objectives}
%Brief description of the overall objectives of your system.
%What your system will be used for? Why is it important? What problems does it solve?


FinTrack is a personal finance management web application designed to help individuals monitor, analyse, and plan their financial situation through a single, unified platform.

The primary objective of the system is to give users full visibility over their financial state. Many people manage money across multiple accounts or wallets without a consolidated view of their overall position. FinTrack addresses this by aggregating all wallets and transactions into a single dashboard that shows total equity, year-to-date expenses, and year-to-date savings at a glance.

A second objective is to support forward-looking financial planning. Knowing the current balance is not enough if a user also wants to understand how their finances may evolve over time. FinTrack therefore provides a forecasting module that projects income, expenses, PAC contributions, and net balance month by month over a 24-month horizon under three scenarios: optimistic, conservative, and stress test. This allows users to anticipate potential shortfalls and make informed decisions before problems arise.

A third objective is to encourage disciplined long-term saving through PAC plans. A PAC (Piano di Accumulo Capitale) is a recurring investment plan in which a fixed amount is contributed each month toward a defined target. FinTrack lets users create, track, and adjust these plans, and integrates them directly into the forecasting logic so that their impact on future balances is always visible.

Finally, the system aims to be accessible even to users who are not yet registered. A public guest area provides three standalone financial calculators — loan amortisation, compound interest, and budget analysis — so that anyone can use the core analytical tools without creating an account. This lowers the barrier to entry and demonstrates the value of the platform before a commitment is made.
