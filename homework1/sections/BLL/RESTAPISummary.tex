\subsection{REST API Summary}

The application exposes two categories of endpoints: Servlet/JSP endpoints using the Post/Redirect/Get pattern for page-based interactions, and REST/JSON endpoints for AJAX-driven features. Both categories are listed below.

\subsubsection*{Servlet/JSP Endpoints (PRG Pattern)}

\begin{longtable}{|p{.30\columnwidth}|p{.08\columnwidth} |p{.40\columnwidth}|p{.12\columnwidth}|}
\hline
\textbf{URI} & \textbf{Method} & \textbf{Description} & \textbf{Filter} \\\hline

\texttt{/} & GET & Home page. & -- \\\hline
\texttt{/login} & GET & Display login page. & -- \\\hline
\texttt{/auth/login} & POST & Authenticate user; redirect to \texttt{/dashboard}. & -- \\\hline
\texttt{/register} & GET & Display registration page. & -- \\\hline
\texttt{/auth/register} & POST & Create new user; redirect to \texttt{/dashboard}. & -- \\\hline
\texttt{/auth/logout} & POST/GET & Invalidate session; redirect to \texttt{/login}. & -- \\\hline

\texttt{/dashboard} & GET & Dashboard with financial summary. & Auth \\\hline

\texttt{/wallets} & GET & List all wallets for the user. & Auth \\\hline
\texttt{/wallets/create} & POST & Create a new wallet; redirect to \texttt{/wallets}. & Auth \\\hline
\texttt{/wallets/edit} & POST & Update wallet; redirect to \texttt{/wallets}. & Auth \\\hline
\texttt{/wallets/delete} & POST & Delete wallet; redirect to \texttt{/wallets}. & Auth \\\hline

\texttt{/pac} & GET & List all PAC plans for the user. & Auth \\\hline
\texttt{/pac/create} & POST & Create a new PAC plan; redirect to \texttt{/pac}. & Auth \\\hline
\texttt{/pac/edit} & POST & Update PAC plan; redirect to \texttt{/pac}. & Auth \\\hline
\texttt{/pac/delete} & POST & Delete PAC plan; redirect to \texttt{/pac}. & Auth \\\hline

\texttt{/guest/report} & GET & Guest calculator page. & Guest \\\hline
\texttt{/report/loan} & POST & Compute loan amortization schedule. & Guest \\\hline
\texttt{/report/compound} & POST & Compute compound interest projection. & Guest \\\hline
\texttt{/report/budget} & POST & Compute budget breakdown. & Guest \\\hline
\texttt{/report/export} & GET & Export report as PDF. & -- \\\hline

\caption{Servlet/JSP endpoints. ``Auth'' = \texttt{AuthenticationFilter} (redirects to \texttt{/login}). ``Guest'' = \texttt{GuestSessionFilter} (initializes guest token).}
\label{tab:servletEndpoints}
\end{longtable}

\subsubsection*{REST/JSON API Endpoints}

\begin{longtable}{|p{.35\columnwidth}|p{.08\columnwidth} |p{.35\columnwidth}|p{.12\columnwidth}|}
\hline
\textbf{URI} & \textbf{Method} & \textbf{Description} & \textbf{Filter} \\\hline

\texttt{/api/transactions?\newline walletId=\{walletId\}} & GET & List all transactions for a wallet. & Auth \\\hline
\texttt{/api/transactions} & POST & Create a new transaction. & Auth \\\hline
\texttt{/api/transactions/\newline\{transactionId\}} & PUT & Update a transaction. & Auth \\\hline
\texttt{/api/transactions/\newline\{transactionId\}} & DELETE & Delete a transaction. & Auth \\\hline

\texttt{/api/expenses} & GET & List all recurring expenses for the user. & Auth \\\hline
\texttt{/api/expenses} & POST & Create a new recurring expense. & Auth \\\hline
\texttt{/api/expenses/\{expenseId\}} & PUT & Update a recurring expense. & Auth \\\hline
\texttt{/api/expenses/\{expenseId\}} & DELETE & Delete a recurring expense. & Auth \\\hline
\texttt{/api/expenses/\newline\{expenseId\}/toggle} & POST & Toggle active/inactive status. & Auth \\\hline

\texttt{/api/forecast?\newline scenario=\{scenario\}} & POST & Compute 24-month projection. & Auth \\\hline
\texttt{/api/forecast?\newline scenario=\{scenario\}} & GET & Retrieve latest stored projection. & Auth \\\hline

\caption{REST/JSON API endpoints. All \texttt{/api/*} routes are protected by \texttt{AuthenticationFilter}, which returns a JSON 401 response for unauthenticated requests instead of redirecting.}
\label{tab:restApiSummary}
\end{longtable}
